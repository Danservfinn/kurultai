# ADR: Hulagu v2 autonomous machine-policy authority

Status: G0 autonomous authority candidate
Date: 2026-07-26

Every predecessor invariant remains binding unless the authority map names an equal-or-stronger successor. Historical approval bytes remain immutable.

Future gate start, activation, pilot, and closure use exact candidate manifests, independent reviews, deterministic default-deny policy admission, closed write sets, RED/GREEN/full-suite receipts, payload/evidence separation, and protected-ref reproduction. Accountable operator identity is provenance only and has no runtime decision authority.

Payments, public posting, identity/SOUL changes, hard deletes, and unapproved outbound email/chat are permanently forbidden. G10/G11 require preexisting consent and communication permission and never auto-invite. G0 activates no runtime and this candidate remains unauthorized pending independent policy review.
